DESTINY CLOUD — FOLLOW-UP ASSETS, CONCEPT 08

SMALL SYMBOL
Native pixel-grid PNGs at 16, 32 and 48px in Ink and transparent versions. Separate compact artwork is used for small sizes instead of simply scaling down the large mark.

FAVICONS
favicon.svg (Ink rounded-square tile), favicon.ico (16/32/48), apple-touch-icon.png (180), icon-192.png, icon-512.png and icon-maskable-512.png plus SVG. Maskable symbol is inset into the centre safe zone.

OUTLINED WORDMARKS
Outlined SVGs: horizontal, stacked and wordmark-only, dark and light backgrounds. Glyphs are vector paths, so fonts need not be installed.
Font used for the outlined version: Destiny = DejaVu Sans ExtraLight (Light-style, approx 300); Cloud = DejaVu Sans Bold (700). These are not Inter outlines. Earlier live-text SVGs used an Inter/Arial fallback stack.

LIGHT CHECK
horizontal_white_120px.png is the 120px-wide horizontal logo on white; the baseline is Ink (#0B0D10) for visibility.

Palette: Ember #FF7A3D; Gold #F5C46B; Ink #0B0D10; Ivory #ECE9E2.
